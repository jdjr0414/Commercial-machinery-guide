About Livestock Equipment Financing

Livestock equipment encompasses one of the broadest equipment categories in agriculture — from $5,000 portable cattle panels to $500,000+ automated poultry house systems. The financing approach varies dramatically by equipment type and whether it is permanently installed (real property) or movable (personal property).

The good news for most livestock equipment buyers: the most commonly purchased equipment — trailers, handling systems, TMR mixers, and feeders — is all movable personal property with clear secondary markets and straightforward lender security interests. Livestock trailers are titled vehicles (same financing process as trucks), squeeze chutes and handling equipment are clearly movable, and TMR mixers are conventional equipment loans.

The more complex financing scenarios involve permanently installed confinement systems for poultry and hogs, where environmental controls, feeding systems, and water lines may become fixtures attached to the building or real estate. For these projects, Farm Credit institutions provide the most complete solution.

Frequently Asked Questions — Livestock Equipment Financing

What livestock equipment is easiest to finance?

Livestock trailers ($15,000–$80,000) and TMR feed mixers ($30,000–$120,000) are the easiest livestock equipment to finance because they are movable, have established secondary markets, and are familiar to most agricultural lenders. Livestock handling systems (squeeze chutes, tubs, alleys, $8,000–$45,000) are also straightforward if they are portable rather than permanently installed. The most challenging to finance are permanently installed poultry house or hog confinement systems ($50,000–$500,000+), which may be classified as real property improvements and require Farm Credit or bank real estate lending.

How do I finance a livestock trailer?

Livestock trailers ($15,000–$80,000 new for 20–53 foot gooseneck designs) are financed as titled vehicles — meaning lenders take a security interest through the vehicle title (similar to a truck) rather than a UCC-1 filing. This makes livestock trailer financing very straightforward: any agricultural lender, most community banks, and many credit unions can finance them. For trailers over $50,000, Farm Credit and independent equipment lenders offer terms up to 60–72 months. For trailers under $30,000, equipment fintech lenders and dealer financing programs are accessible options. WW Manufacturing and Featherlite are the most widely recognized brands for lenders.

Can I finance a complete squeeze chute and cattle handling system?

Yes — portable squeeze chutes and handling systems ($8,000–$45,000) are financed as personal property equipment. Brands like WW Manufacturing, Powder River, Hi-Qual, and Arrowquip are well-known to agricultural lenders. For a complete portable handling system (chute, tub, alleys, load-out, $20,000–$45,000), most agricultural equipment lenders and Farm Credit will approve straightforwardly. For permanently installed concrete and steel handling facilities that are part of the real property, Farm Credit's real estate lending capacity is needed. Expect 36–60 month terms and 10–20% down through most independent lenders.

What are the financing options for poultry or hog confinement equipment?

Poultry house equipment systems ($50,000–$500,000+) and hog confinement systems are complex financing projects because they often involve both equipment and building/real property components. Feed lines, waterers, ventilation systems, and environmental controls are equipment; the building structure is real property. Farm Credit institutions are by far the best lenders for confinement system projects because they can handle both components. USDA FSA guaranteed loans are also available for beginning farmers or operations needing additional support. Big Dutchman, Cumberland (CTB), Ziggity Systems, and Chore-Time are the major equipment brands.

How do TMR feed mixers work as collateral for financing?

TMR (Total Mixed Ration) feed mixers ($30,000–$120,000) are excellent collateral for equipment financing. They are clearly personal property (movable), have established secondary markets, and are recognized by virtually all agricultural equipment lenders. Vertical TMR mixers (Kuhn Knight, DeLaval, Trioliet) and horizontal mixers are both commonly financed. Used TMR mixers are actively traded because growing livestock operations frequently upgrade capacity. Expect 36–72 month terms and 10–20% down through independent lenders, or potential 0% down bundled with a tractor purchase through OEM programs.

Can I finance automatic calf feeders?

Yes — automatic calf feeders ($5,000–$20,000 per unit) are financed as equipment. Most automated calf feeding systems for a 100–200 calf operation run $25,000–$60,000 for the complete installation. Major brands include DeLaval, Lely, Förster-Technik, and Urban. Automatic calf feeders reduce labor requirements significantly (one unit can manage 30–40 calves vs. twice-daily manual feeding) and are viewed as productivity investments that justify financing. For amounts under $25,000, fintech lenders and dealer financing are the most accessible options. For complete systems over $25,000, standard agricultural equipment lenders apply.
